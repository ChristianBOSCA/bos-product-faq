How to use this tool

1. Search any product name or SKU — typos are fine — and pick it. The product name links out to its live page on the site.

2. You'll see its variants/SKUs, weights, and manual links. The list opens on All variants (the whole product); click any SKU to see only that variant's questions.

3. Where a question lands — whole product vs. one SKU: with All variants selected, + Log question files it under the whole product (the "umbrella"), so it shows for every variant. Click a specific SKU first and the question is tagged to just that variant. If the tool detects an attachment-specific question, it'll also offer to scope it for you.

4. New questions land in Unanswered. Anyone who knows the answer types it in — it moves to Pending approval with their initials and the date.

5. How to approve (two ways): on the Pending approval list, tick the checkboxes and hit ✓ Approve selected to clear a batch at once — or hit ✓ Approve on any single question. Both work the same; use whichever suits. Editing an approved answer sends it back for re-approval.

6. Visibility — important. Every question carries a tag: ✅ Customer-approved (safe to reuse in customer-facing copy, help centre, macros), 🔒 Internal only (never send to customers — margins, supplier info, workarounds), or ◻ Not set. Other projects and AI agents may only pull Customer-approved answers, so anything left unset is treated as internal. Mark it via the buttons on each question, or select several and use the bulk buttons.

7. Working at the same time as others is safe. When someone hits Edit or Move, that question locks for everyone else (you'll see “🔒 AB is editing”) and unlocks when they save or cancel. The list auto-refreshes, and if two people change the same thing the second save is stopped rather than overwriting.

8. ✨ Generate / Polish turns your rough notes into a customer-ready answer. Type the facts, click it, then review before saving — it only uses what you typed.

Tip: your name is remembered on this device. Use “not you?” in the top corner to switch. Use the General & Policies entry for shipping/returns/process questions that aren't product-specific.
